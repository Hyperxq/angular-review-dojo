# L1 - Product badge

**Reported by:** QA  |  **Area:** Product badge  |  **Priority:** Medium

## What we see

1. The little category tag next to the product name is always empty.
2. "20% off" appears only for the product the page loaded with. Moving to another product with few units left does
   not show it, and moving from one with few units to a well-stocked one keeps showing it.
3. If I open "Details" and then go to the next product, the details panel stays open.
4. Pressing "Sell one" until only 2 units are left never shows "20% off".

## Expected

The badge always describes the product it is given.
