# S2 - Variant picker

**Reported by:** QA  |  **Area:** Product page  |  **Priority:** Medium

## What we see

On the product page, pick the "Limited edition" variant of a keyboard, then follow a link to
another product (for example a mouse). The new product opens with "Limited edition" already
selected and the price shown includes the surcharge, even though I never chose it for this product.
Reloading the page shows the right default.

## Expected

Every product starts with its standard variant selected.
