# L2 - Product detail

**Reported by:** QA  |  **Area:** Catalog  |  **Priority:** High

## What we see

- Clicking quickly from one product to another (or hitting the browser back/forward buttons in
  a row) sometimes leaves me on a page whose title is a product I did not ask for last. It happens
  more often on a slow connection.
- Sometimes the "Related products" list belongs to a different category than the product shown
  above it.
- Reloading the page always shows the right data.

## Expected

The page always shows the product I navigated to last, with its own category and related products.
